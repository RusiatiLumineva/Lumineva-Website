How Every Build Works

02

Build

I build only the agreed stage, inside the tools you already use. You review it once, and I complete one round of revisions.

Questions, Answered

I am not sure which build I need. Where should I start?

Tell me what is happening now through the contact form. I will suggest where to start, usually the stage where potential clients drop off fastest. If you need more than one build, we start with that stage.

Why are the prices shown as "from"?

Every coaching business is set up a little differently. I confirm the price after a short review of your current process. It goes above the starting price only when the stage is more complex, for example several entry points, a move from an existing system, or more than one person running it.

When does the timeline start?

The timeline starts when I have the access and information the build needs, not when the agreement is signed. Your review and the revision round both happen inside that timeline.

How does payment work?

A 30% deposit when you accept the proposal, so the work can start. 50% when the draft build is delivered for your review. The final 20% after revisions and handover.

Can I book more than one build?

Yes, one at a time. I complete and hand over the first build, then we scope the next stage. This keeps each build focused and on schedule.

What is not included?

Brand or visual design, website pages, marketing copy, content strategy, offer or pricing design, moving old client data, and ongoing maintenance. Each build ends with a full handover, so you can run the system yourself.

How is this different from hiring a virtual assistant?

A virtual assistant usually completes the tasks you assign. My work focuses on the structure behind those tasks. I map the workflow, clarify ownership, define next actions, document the process, and build a system that is easier to follow and hand over.

What tools do you work with?

I work with tools such as Notion, Google Workspace, ClickUp, Calendly, Tally, MailerLite, Systeme.io, Zoho, and other tools already used in your business. I start with the workflow rather than forcing a new platform.
