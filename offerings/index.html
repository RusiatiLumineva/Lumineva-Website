Questions, Answered

How is this different from hiring a virtual assistant?

A virtual assistant usually completes the tasks you assign. My work focuses on the structure behind those tasks. I help map the workflow, clarify ownership, define next actions, document the process, and build a system that is easier to follow and hand over.

Do you only work with coaches?

My current focus is solo coaches and small coaching businesses, especially those with an established paid offer, active clients, regular inquiries, or visible growth. The work is centered on making the journey from inquiry to booking and onboarding clearer.

How does the partnership actually work?

We begin by understanding how your current process works and where the friction is. From there, we agree on a clear scope, build the relevant workflow, document the important steps, and make ownership and handover clear.

What tools do you work with?

I can work with tools such as Notion, Google Workspace, ClickUp, Calendly, Tally, MailerLite, Systeme.io, Zoho, and other tools already used in your business. I start with the workflow rather than forcing a new platform.

I am not sure which offering I need, what should I do?

Send an inquiry describing the part of your client journey that feels unclear or too manual. I can help you determine whether an Inquiry Path Build, Booking Path Build, Client Onboarding Build, Clarity Session, or another project scope makes the most sense.
