The Philosophy

Structures that Actually Hold

Rusiati — Operations and Systems Partner

Where Operational Discipline
Meets Intentional Coaching Work

Lumineva Aurorae supports solo coaches and small coaching businesses that are doing meaningful work, but still managing too much of their client journey manually.

When inquiries are scattered across different places, follow-ups depend on memory, bookings require repeated messages, and onboarding is handled differently each time, the client journey becomes harder to manage than it needs to be.

I help coaches bring structure to these important operational paths. That includes the journey from inquiry to next action, from interest to booking, and from payment to a clear client onboarding experience.

My background is in finance operations and enterprise systems. I have more than a decade of experience in finance operations and five years of experience supporting SAP systems for a manufacturing company in Indonesia.

That experience shaped how I work today. I pay attention to process clarity, accurate information, clear ownership, reliable handovers, and the small points of friction that make a workflow difficult to follow.

How I Work

I do not begin by adding more tools. I begin by understanding how your client journey works today.

I look at how inquiries are received, how follow-ups are managed, how bookings are confirmed, and what happens after a client decides to work with you.

From there, I help identify unclear ownership, repeated manual tasks, missing next actions, and steps that exist only in your memory.

The result is a practical workflow with defined next actions, clear responsibility, relevant SOPs, and handover documentation that can be understood and used.

The goal is not to create a perfect system. The goal is to make the work clearer, more repeatable, and easier to manage as your coaching business grows.

Who This Is For

Ready to build something
that actually works?

This work is for solo coaches and small coaching businesses with an established paid offer, active clients, regular inquiries, or visible signs of growth.

You may already have the right tools, but the process between inquiry, booking, payment, onboarding, and the first session still depends heavily on you.

You do not necessarily need more software or more hustle. You need clarity on how the work flows, what happens next, who owns each step, and what needs to be documented.

I support this through focused Inquiry Path Builds, Booking Path Builds, and Client Onboarding Builds, designed around how your coaching business actually works.
